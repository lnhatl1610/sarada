# Storefront guides

## `/how-to-order` — buying guide
- A Read-mode page in the existing storefront palette with a short introduction and a numbered, linear purchase flow.
- Link to product discovery, cart, and order tracking at the relevant steps.
- Explain that currently enabled payment methods and optional coupon entry are shown at checkout; do not promise an unavailable method or fixed shipping price.
- Provide footer and sitemap links to the guide and to the care guide.
- Keep the content readable on mobile and keyboard navigation visible.

## `/care-guide` — garment care
- A Read-mode page with native, keyboard-operable disclosures grouped by cotton, wool, silk, denim, and leather.
- Identify all material advice as general guidance; the specific garment's care label and product instructions take precedence.
- Link to the help center for questions. Do not replace product-specific care instructions with generic guidance.

## `/size-guide` — size guide
- Load the published CMS page with slug `size-guide`, using its title, body, and SEO metadata.
- Keep product-specific size charts and recommendations on the product page; do not invent measurements when store content is missing.
- Link to the guide from the customer support footer and HTML sitemap.
